OFFICE HQ · MAC INSTALLER

Install or update Office HQ on your Mac

Use your iPhone or iPad to send the latest published Office HQ Mac installer through AirDrop or Messages. Open the link on your Mac to install Office HQ or update an existing installation.

↓ Download Mac ZIP

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, tap Send latest Mac installer, choose AirDrop, and select your Mac. You can also share the link through Messages.
  2. On your Mac, open the received link and download Office_HQ_for_Mac.zip.
  3. Double-click the ZIP, then open Office HQ.app. macOS may require you to review and explicitly open the unsigned application.
  4. On a first install, approve setup. If Office HQ is already installed, select Update Office HQ when prompted. Existing workspace data is preserved and replaced application files are backed up. The updater checks the new files and safely restarts only a verified Office HQ local server. If another program occupies the port, close it manually and reopen the app.
Important: This is an unsigned Mac launcher, not an offline App Store application or a remote installer. On first setup, it can install the independent local Ollama runtime and llama3.2:3b model, requiring several GB of disk space, internet and macOS 14+. Reopening the launcher checks the published code and requests consent before replacing existing application files; models and company records are not automatically deleted. To synchronize assignments, sign in to the same Office HQ Cloud account under Company Messages on the Mac and iPhone/iPad. The Mac can draft with Ollama only for explicitly authorized employees. Coding tasks include: separately approve a repository, source files and checks, then inspect the resulting diff. Configure a tool-capable local model, or explicitly enable OpenRouter Inkling for public tasks. Account permissions remain separate. iPhone/iPad sync runs while its Office HQ web app is open.
