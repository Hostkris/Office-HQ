OFFICE HQ · MAC INSTALLER

Send Office HQ to your Mac

Use your iPhone or iPad to share the Office HQ Mac download link through AirDrop or Messages. The Mac downloads and opens the installer—no remote control or Mac sign-in is required.

↓ Download Mac ZIP

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, tap Send to Mac (AirDrop), choose AirDrop, and select your Mac. You can also share the link through Messages.
  2. On your Mac, open the received link and download Office_HQ_for_Mac.zip.
  3. Double-click the ZIP, then open Office HQ.app. macOS may require you to review and explicitly open the unsigned application.
  4. Approve setup on your Mac. Office HQ installs the independent Ollama engine and the llama3.2:3b local model, then opens your Mac workspace. A large model download is required.
Important: This is an unsigned Mac launcher, not an offline App Store application or a remote installer. After your approval, it installs the full independent local Ollama runtime and downloads its llama3.2:3b model, requiring several GB of disk space, internet, and macOS 14+. To synchronize assignments, sign in to the same Office HQ Cloud account under Company Messages on the Mac and iPhone/iPad. The Mac can draft with Ollama only for explicitly authorized employees; outside account actions remain disabled. iPhone/iPad sync runs while its Office HQ web app is open.
